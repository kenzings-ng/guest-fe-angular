Fewer things. Made to last.

Maison started in a single room above a bookshop, with one question: what if we made less, but made it properly?

How it started

No warehouse, no investors, no twelve-collections-a-year treadmill — just a small room above a secondhand bookshop and a conviction that most clothing is made to be replaced, not worn. We wanted to build the opposite: pieces you'd still reach for a decade in.

That's still how we work. Two collections a year, not twelve. Every fabric sourced from mills we've partnered with for over a decade, and every pattern tested through a full season of real wear before it ever reaches a rack.
