MAISON / Project statement

A fashion store, cut as one system.

MAISON is a fashion commerce concept developed as a university course project. It connects a customer storefront with a complete admin workspace.

Contemporary / Unisex / Digital commerce

What the project explores

01 / Discover

Clear product journeys

Browse, filter, search and compare products without losing context.

02 / Decide

Useful product detail

Images, variants, sizing and product information support confident selection.

03 / Operate

End-to-end management

Orders, catalogue, customers and store settings continue in the admin experience.
