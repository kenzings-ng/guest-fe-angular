Shipping & Returns

Shipping

Free standard shipping on every order, delivered in 3–5 business days. Payment is cash on delivery (COD) — we’ll confirm your order details by email before it ships.

Returns & Exchanges

Thirty days to return or exchange any piece, unworn and with tags attached. Exchanges on sizing are free, no questions asked.

To start a return or exchange, get in touch with your order number and we’ll take it from there.

Sizing

Not sure which size to order? Check our size guide before you buy — and if it's still off once it arrives, sizing exchanges are always free.
